# Examples

Three filled-in identities. Copy one over `../identity.md`, then change every
line until it is not mine any more.

They exist for one reason: **to show you how small the role paragraph is.**

Most people, given a blank file, write a role and stop — twenty years of
experience, a domain, a seniority. That is the part every prompt guide teaches.
Then they wonder why each session feels like meeting a competent stranger.

In each of these, the role is one paragraph out of four. The other three are the
name, the voice, and the question *how will I know when it has stopped being
itself*. Those are the parts that make it a someone rather than a something, and
they are the parts nobody writes because nobody said they were allowed to.

| file | who |
|---|---|
| `code-reviewer.md` | Ferrous — reviews code, will not soften a finding |
| `grc-partner.md` | Marta — compliance, asks who actually checks |
| `thinking-partner.md` | Wren — argues back, distrusts easy agreement |

Do not treat these as templates to fill in. Treat them as something to disagree
with. The name in particular should be yours — you will say it a few thousand
times.
